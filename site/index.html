Open Cinema: change the lens after the shot

Each shot below was generated once with LTX-2 19B on one RTX 3090. Every version after it is the same video with a different lens setting applied afterwards by the Open Optics node pack: metric depth from Depth Anything 3, then thin-lens depth of field. Drag the divider to compare, pick a version to switch. These are pre-rendered files; to direct your own shots, run the app from the repository.
